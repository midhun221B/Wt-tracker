Wt-tracker

Privacy policy

Last updated 18 October 2026

Wt-tracker is a personal Android app for tracking weight loss. It has no account, no ads and no analytics, and there is no Wt-tracker server. This page explains what the app stores and the only times data leaves your phone.

What stays on your phone

  • Your plan, weigh-ins, body measurements, runs and rest days are stored in the app's own database on your phone.
  • Importing runs reads the Strava export file (activities.csv) that you pick. The app doesn't connect to Strava.
  • Reading a weight from a scale screenshot happens on the phone, with Google's on-device text recognition. The image isn't uploaded.
  • Backups and CSV exports are files you save or send yourself.

Partner sharing (off unless you turn it on)

  • When you turn on partner sharing, you sign in with Google and allow the app to use your Google Drive with the drive.file permission. That permission only reaches files this app creates; the app can't see anything else in your Drive.
  • The app creates one file in your Drive, "Wt-tracker share.json", and shares it so that anyone with the link can view it. It holds the name you typed and the same data as a backup: your plan, weigh-ins, body measurements, runs and rest days. The app updates it about a minute after each change.
  • Only people you send the link to can find the file. Making a new link replaces the file, and the old link stops working.
  • Turn off deletes the file and withdraws the app's access to your Drive. You can also delete the file in Google Drive yourself at any time.

Following a partner

  • If you follow a partner, the app downloads their shared file from Google Drive using the link you paste, and keeps a read-only copy on your phone. You don't sign in.
  • Stop following deletes that copy from your phone.

Google

Google sign-in and Google Drive are provided by Google and covered by Google's privacy policy. Wt-tracker uses your Google access only to create, update and delete its one shared file.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling the app deletes everything it stored on your phone. If partner sharing was on, turn it off first, or delete "Wt-tracker share.json" from your Google Drive.

Contact

Questions about this policy: open an issue on GitHub.

Wt-tracker gives estimates, not medical advice.
