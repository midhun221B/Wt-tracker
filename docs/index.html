Downhill

Every week, a little further down the road. A personal Android weight-loss tracker. It puts your plan next to an honest forecast built from your own weigh-ins and runs.

What it does

  • One weigh-in a week, on the day you choose, and a realistic trend with a range and an expected date.
  • Runs from Strava's export file, with a weekly run streak.
  • Estimates of how much to eat to stay on plan. It never suggests below 1,800 kcal a day or more than 1 kg a week.
  • Optional partner sharing: your partner follows your progress on their own phone, read-only.

Your data stays yours

No account, no ads, no analytics. Everything lives on your phone unless you turn on partner sharing. Read the privacy policy.
